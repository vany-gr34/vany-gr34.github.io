crime_video_benchmark.exeGitHub ↗

Criminal Behavior Detection — Video Model Benchmark

A comparative video-classification study across 3,274 videos pooled from three public datasets. I evaluated seven model implementations using stratified splits, pretrained weights where available, fine-tuning and held-out testing.

MViT-v2-S achieved 90.04% test accuracy and 0.898 macro-F1 on 492 held-out clips. The leading models’ confidence intervals overlap, so the comparison does not establish a definitive winner. The SlowFast- and X3D-inspired entries are custom fallback networks, not the original architectures.

Harris Hawks Optimization for Combinatorial Problems

Adapted continuous Harris Hawks Optimization to permutation-based combinatorial problems: the Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP) and Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP). The implementation explores continuous-to-discrete transformations, permutation repair, exploration and exploitation strategies, and Lévy-flight dives.

04 Research Interests

metaheuristics.txt

Optimization algorithms. I am interested in how metaheuristics behave on discrete and constrained problems, how to design fair baseline comparisons, and how evaluation budgets and stochastic variation affect conclusions.

math_for_ai.txt

Mathematics for machine learning. Probability, statistics, linear algebra and optimization: the ideas that explain how models learn, how uncertainty should be interpreted, and when a result is reliable.

core_ai.txt

Reliable applied AI. Model evaluation, forecasting, retrieval quality and explainability. I am interested in systems that distinguish evidence from generated text and make limitations visible to users.

open_questions.txt

Questions I like to chase

How can we compare stochastic optimization algorithms fairly when the objective is noisy and evaluation budgets are limited?

How should a model communicate uncertainty instead of presenting every prediction as equally reliable?

How can retrieval and language models make analytical results easier to interpret without replacing the underlying evidence?
